Nice to meet you

A quiet roost for your Claude Code sessions.

Perch sits in your system tray and keeps an eye on every running session — surfacing status, usage and nudges as a small desktop overlay, so you don't have to keep flipping back to check.

  • 👁️It watches, you work. A glance tells you which sessions are running, waiting on you, or stuck.
  • 🔔It taps you on the shoulder. Optional toasts and chimes when a run finishes or needs input.
  • 🎚️It grows with you. Perch has a lot under the hood. We'll start light and you can add more whenever.
