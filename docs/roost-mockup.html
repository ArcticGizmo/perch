Command center

A tmux-style view of every Claude Code session at once. It opens from a glyph beside + New session. Each session gets a live pane. Sessions Perch controls can be answered and driven from their pane. Terminal and IDE sessions are tailed read-only. Anything blocked on you, or finished and unreviewed, stands out on the pane, in the rail, and on the overlay glyph.

A split-panes glyph on the New session row

The window · interactive

Rail for urgency, panes that stay put

The rail on the left ranks sessions by how much they need you. The panes keep the order they arrived in, so a status change never moves a pane out from under someone typing. Tiled shows four panes at a time and scrolls a row at a time. A pill counts panes out of view and turns amber when one of them needs you. Try it: allow the permission in perch-api, reply in a Perch pane, switch layouts, or press . to jump to the next session that needs you.

◆ Perch-controlled pane

  • Source: the in-memory conversation, so it streams token by token.
  • Permission, question and plan cards can be answered right in the pane.
  • Footer: a compact composer (Enter sends, Esc interrupts) and ⤢ to open the full session window.

▣ Terminal / IDE pane

  • Source: the transcript file, tailed from its last byte offset.
  • Blocked prompts are shown but can only be answered in the terminal.
  • Footer: Focus terminal ↗, and Take over in Perch to hand off with --resume.
Build order

Three phases, read-only first

Phase 1 meets three of the four asks on its own. Interactivity comes second because it depends on reusing the session window's composer, the riskiest piece to extract.

PHASE 1

Read-only command center

Window, urgency rail, three layouts, status chrome, byte-offset transcript tailing for every pane, the overlay glyph with its badge, acknowledge on focus, Focus terminal.

Meets: history + tailing, attention states, glyph
PHASE 2

Interactive Perch panes

Bind the live conversation, answer permission/question/plan cards in the pane, a lite composer, interrupt, open the full window, and no duplicate toasts for a pane already on screen.

Meets: interactive Perch sessions
PHASE 3

Polish

Share the full composer (slash palette, @-mentions, history). Add Take over in Perch, taskbar flash, a global hotkey, pinning and closing panes, and a "load earlier" affordance.

For the requester

Ended sessions?Proposed: they stay greyed out for 10 minutes, then drop. The History window keeps the rest.

More than six sessions?Tiled shows four at a time and scrolls to six. Past six, the quietest collapse into the strip under the grid, as scratch does here.

Terminal sessions stay read-only.There is no control channel into a terminal claude. Is Take over in Perch enough?
