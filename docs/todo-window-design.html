1

Left: triage

A title-first composer, then the list grouped Overdue → Upcoming → No date. Rows are compact — circle, title, relative due — because the detail lives on the right now.

2

Right: the reading pane

Click a row and it opens here: the title large, the due time as a status pill with its absolute date, and the full notes at a comfortable reading size — the point of the change.

3

Complete from the circle

The circle on each row is the click target to complete — it strikes through and drops into Completed. The rest of the row selects it into the pane.

4

Edit in place

Edit turns the reading pane into a form (title, notes, due) with Save / Cancel — you edit where you were reading, and the composer stays purely for adding new ones.

5

Empty & completed

Nothing selected → the pane invites you to pick one. Done items fold behind the Show completed count so the list stays about what's left.

6

Two-part due read

Relative time carries urgency (overdue 20m, in 2h); the absolute date sits beside it, quiet, for when "in 3d" isn't precise enough.

The overlay section

On the overlay the Todo section behaves like Friends: a header you can collapse, with a + on the right to add without opening the window first. Hypertree gains the same collapse.

Interactions

Window

  • Click a row — open it in the reading pane on the right.
  • Click the circle — complete (strike + move to Completed).
  • Edit (in the pane) — swap to a form; Save / Cancel.
  • Add due date — the chip expands to date + time; a bare date defaults to 9am.
  • Delete lives in the pane; Esc closes the window.

Overlay section

  • Click the header — collapse / expand (remembered across restarts).
  • Click + — open Todos ready to add.
  • Click a line — open the list; right-click — Complete.
  • Collapsed still shows the open count, so it stays glanceable.
  • Hypertree gets the identical collapsible header.
